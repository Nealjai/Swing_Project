Understanding the Screener Logic

Active Engine

The screener adapts to market regime by activating one engine. The active engine is highlighted below.

Bull Engine Weak Engine

The Bull Engine seeks strong stocks breaking out from sound price structures. The Weak Engine targets defensive, oversold rebound setups.

Tag Definitions

Tags are assigned from normalized scores (0 to 1) in table logic. A stock can carry both 🏆 and ⚡ together.

🏆 Leadership
Given when leadership_score ≥ 0.90.
⚡ Actionable
Given when actionability_score ≥ 0.58.
👀 Watchlist
Given when 0.50 ≤ actionability_score < 0.58 and ⚡ is not assigned.
- (None)
Shown when none of the above thresholds are met.

How scores are calculated

All components are normalized with a robust method (median/MAD + sigmoid) before weighting.

  • Bull Leadership = 0.60 × RS + 0.40 × Trend
  • Bull Actionability = 0.40 × Breakout + 0.25 × Compression + 0.20 × Volume + 0.15 × Stage
  • Weak Leadership = 0.70 × Trend + 0.30 × Liquidity
  • Weak Actionability = 0.45 × Reversal + 0.35 × Extension + 0.20 × Capitulation
  • Final Score: Bull 100 × (0.55 × Actionability + 0.45 × Leadership); Weak 100 × (0.60 × Actionability + 0.40 × Leadership)

The scanner’s first job is to determine the overall market health. We do this by comparing SPY’s close with its 200-day simple moving average.
